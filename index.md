# VoiceMaestro
Dynamic voice channels for Discord — simple for members, powerful for admins.

VoiceMaestro creates temporary voice channels on demand (Join-to-Create), then gives channel owners a clean interface to manage them: lock, hide, rename, limits, permits, and more.

## Why servers use it
- **Auto channel creation:** Join the hub → get your own channel instantly
- **Owner controls:** lock/unlock, hide/reveal, rename, limits
- **Access tools:** permit, reject, block/unblock
- **Ownership tools:** claim, transfer, trust helpers
- **Optional UI:** buttons + dashboards (where enabled)
