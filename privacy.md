## Privacy Policy

VoiceMaestro processes Discord server/member metadata necessary to operate temporary voice room features.

### Data used

- Server, channel, role, and user identifiers
- Voice room ownership/control metadata
- Command usage metadata
- Optional operational logging metadata

### Why data is used

- Create and manage temporary rooms
- Enforce owner access controls
- Support moderation and troubleshooting
- Improve reliability and service health

### Data sharing

Data is not sold. Limited processing may occur through required infrastructure providers.
